Who We Are

Welcome to Selbora, a premium epoxy furniture brand proudly operating under Sifan Fibrotech. We specialize in designing and manufacturing high-quality epoxy table tops, custom furniture, and modern decorative creations that combine craftsmanship with contemporary design. Our passion lies in transforming ordinary wood and extraordinary statement pieces that bring elegance, creativity, and character into every space.


At Selbora, we believe furniture is more than just a product — it is a reflection of style and creativity. Every epoxy table we create is carefully crafted with attention to detail, premium materials, and durable finishing. Since every wood texture and resin flow is unique, each table carries its own individual character, making every piece truly one of a kind.


We design furniture for homes, offices, cafes, restaurants, and modern interior spaces. From luxury river tables to custom epoxy table tops, our products are made to enhance interiors with a stylish and contemporary appearance. We also offer customized designs where customers can choose sizes, colors, patterns, and styles according to their preferences.


As a brand under Sifan Fibrotech, we focus on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction. Our team continuously works on new ideas and modern style techniques to create products that are both visually attractive and long-lasting.


What makes Selbora unique is our commitment to craftsmanship and originality. Every product is designed with precision to ensure a premium finish and lasting durability. By blending artistic product work with natural materials, we create furniture that becomes the centerpiece of any environment.


At Selbora, we are dedicated to delivering modern epoxy furniture that combines elegance, durability, and creativity in every design.
